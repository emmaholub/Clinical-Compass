Clinical Compass

A safer starting point after a new diagnosis

Emma Holub

The problem

Patients face unfamiliar language, scattered evidence, and short appointments. Search results do not clearly separate guidance, safety warnings, and advice.

The solution

One calm flow: plain-language overview → guideline-linked treatment options → traceable safety evidence → questions for the doctor → recruiting US trials.

What is different

Not generic chat and not chat with a PDF: disease-specific guidance drives retrieval, exact quotes are checked against source URLs, and missing evidence remains visible.
